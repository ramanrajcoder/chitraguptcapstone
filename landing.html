One source of truth

Numbers that always agree

Most small-business tools calculate cash, profit and balances separately on every screen — so they drift apart. ChitRagupt posts every sale, payment, return and expense to a single ledger, and every report reads from it.

  • Credit sales are never counted as cash received
  • Customer receipts and supplier payments flow into the cash book
  • Supplier payables and customer advances show as liabilities
  • Built-in reconciliation checks warn you the moment something is off

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the app in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) on your computer or phone.

Where is my data stored?

In your account's secure online database (Neon Postgres), so it is available on every device you log in from. Your browser also keeps an offline copy while you work. We still recommend downloading a backup file from Settings now and then as an extra safety copy.

Can I use it for GST invoices?

Yes. Each product carries its GST rate, invoices show GST per line and in total, and the GST dashboard summarises output versus input tax. Please confirm filing requirements with your accountant.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes — switch between English and हिंदी from the top bar for menus, page titles and most labels. More languages can be added.

What happens if I make a mistake on a bill?

Void it. Stock is restored and all accounting effects are reversed, but the bill stays on record, marked VOID, with an entry in the audit log. For returns, issue a credit note.

Can I move my data to another device?

Yes. Just log in with the same login id and password on the other device and your data is there. Data from an older, browser-only version can be imported once after you create your account.
